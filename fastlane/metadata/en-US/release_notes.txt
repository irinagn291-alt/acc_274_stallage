Version 1.0 introduces Stallage, an on-device crib for production and costume gear.

• Inventory with stall chips, Tag status filters, Next scan guidance, Trail, and Scan to seat or hop
• Scan into an open stall with camera or Type or paste a code; Name this Tag after a new seat
• Lifecycle with Overdue, Hops, Trail, Next hop, Hop trail, and Seated now
• Settings for Stalls, Add a stall, Stall then hop, Contact, Re-run onboarding, and Reset all data
• Onboarding covering stalls, seats, hops, Lent, overdue, freeze, and on-device QR
• Camera permission for barcode and QR reading; notifications may be requested near launch
