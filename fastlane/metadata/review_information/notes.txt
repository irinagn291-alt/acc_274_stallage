Camera permission is requested the first time Scan reaches the pre-permission screen and the user taps Continue. The system usage string is: Stallage uses the camera to read a barcode or QR on a tagged tool, cable, camera, or costume so it can be seated in a stall. If denied, the app shows Camera is off with Open Settings, and typing a code remains available. A system notification permission prompt may appear near cold launch with no custom in-app explanation screen. Photos, microphone, location, and tracking are not requested.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no public or shared user-generated content feed, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Stallage is a dark, on-device crib for production and costume gear. Belongings live in named stalls as Tags. It is for people who need to know where a tool, cable, camera, or costume sits and who last took it. No credentials or demo account are needed.

Cold launch shows a brief dark screen. If onboarding is unfinished, a full-screen cover appears that cannot be dismissed by swipe. Use Next on pages 1 through 3, Continue on page 4, or Skip on any page. Continue or Skip finishes onboarding; if there are no stalls yet, Bench, Crew hold, and Repair pane are created. The main shell shows the segmented control Inventory, Lifecycle, and Settings (accessibility label Crib). On Inventory, select a stall chip so Scan is enabled, then tap Scan. On the Scan cover, tap Continue if shown for camera permission, or use Type or paste a code and Seat or hop. After seating an unknown code, Name this Tag may appear; use Continue or Later. To hop, open another stall and scan a known Tag. Open Lifecycle for overdue Issued Tags and hop trail. Open Settings to Add a stall, use Show stall QR from Inventory when a stall is open, open Stall then hop, Contact, Re-run onboarding, or Reset all data.

Inventory, stalls, Tags, hops, trail, search, and QR remain on this device. Settings Contact opens the support page.

There is no region lock. The UI is English only; dates follow the device locale.

No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies. The app is a personal on-device crib for gear Tags and does not provide professional certification, medical, or regulated services.

The app is a personal log only and gives no recommendations. It shows no health, medical, fitness, or product-nutrition information.

The app scans barcodes and QR codes. A scannable QR (qr-code.png) is attached to this review; it encodes Body 5D. Simulator users type Body 5D in Type or paste a code, then Seat or hop.
